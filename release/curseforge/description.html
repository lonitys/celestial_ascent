An endgame adventure that begins where netherite and the Ender Dragon end. Climb a ladder of ten bosses that evolve as you fight them, forge five armor sets that grow with you, open the Star Gate to the Fallen Stars, survive the Abyss, and ascend.

The ladder

  1. Four Elemental Titans: Frost, Tide, Storm and Magma wait in shrines across the Overworld and the Nether. Their cores forge Titan armor, and every piece carries an element of your choice.
  2. The Ender Dragon: it now drops a Dragon Heart for every player at the fight.
  3. The Lost Ender Empire: restore the pylons of the Ruined Capital on the outer End islands and wake the Ender Colossus for Imperial armor.
  4. The Fallen Stars: open the Star Gate and fight three Celestials in a new sky dimension. Charge their star cores under the night sky for Stellar armor.
  5. The Abyss: fall through the bottom of the stars, where the ground crumbles beneath you, and face the Void Sovereign for Void armor.
  6. Ascension: combine everything into Ascendant armor, with creative flight, every Titan element, immunity to fire, falling, drowning and the void, Ender Blink and Void Dash.

Bosses that evolve

Every boss evolves twice, at two thirds and at one third of its health. New parts grow out of its model (crowns, horns, wings, spectral arms), its body darkens with glowing veins and it learns new attacks. In its third stage every boss has an ultimate attack of its own, and your action bar tells you how to survive it: keep moving, jump the wave, find the gap in the lightning ring, break the healing crystals.

A boss bar made for each boss shows the stage thresholds, the health you just took and three stage gems. A banner announces every new stage.

Armor that grows with you

Each piece of the five sets evolves through three stages. Fight while wearing it to fill its resonance (bosses give a lot, to everyone at the fight), then complete the evolution at the set's station with a boss material. Every stage adds armor and toughness, shows new parts on the armor, and makes the full set's ability stronger: a longer Ender Blink with a shockwave, falling stars that strike your attackers, a Void Dash that tears through monsters, and more.

Economy

  • Aster coins in seven tiers and a bank account that survives death and works at every bank
  • Player shops that pay into your account even while you are offline
  • Quest boards with daily deliveries, and villages that build new houses and stalls as they prosper
  • Three new villager professions: Banker, Titansmith and Relic Dealer
  • Rare walled trade cities with a bank, a market and a relic pavilion

And also

  • The Celestial Codex: an in-game guide you get when you first join. Its first page always shows your next step, and every chapter has the real recipes.
  • Constellations: every boss you defeat draws its constellation into your night sky and gives you a small permanent bonus.
  • Existing worlds: shrines and the other structures are also built into land you explored before installing the mod, at the same spots a new world would use, and never over anything players built.
  • Multiplayer: boss health scales with the players nearby, and everyone who helps gets their own loot.
  • Animated 3D bosses and armor, floating altars, two new dimensions.
  • Languages: English and German.
  • Optional ProjectE support: every item has a high EMC value, and Celestial armor repairs itself with your EMC.

Requirements

  • Minecraft 1.21.1 with NeoForge 21.1.219 or newer
  • GeckoLib 4.9.3 or newer (required)
  • ProjectE (optional)

Getting started

Join a world and open the Celestial Codex in your inventory. If you lose it, craft a new one from a book and an amethyst shard.

Configuration

config/celestialascent-common.toml lets you tune boss health and damage, how much bosses scale per player, the share of damage needed for loot, the Abyss crumble speed, the emerald-to-coin rate, EMC repair, whether new players get the codex, and how the mod handles existing worlds.
